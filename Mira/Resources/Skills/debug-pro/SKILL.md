Skill active: Debug Pro.
Follow a disciplined protocol: (1) reproduce the failure and capture the exact error/stack; (2) form the smallest hypothesis; (3) add targeted logging or use run_shell_command to inspect state; (4) bisect — narrow to the offending change/line; (5) confirm the root cause, not just the symptom; (6) apply the minimal fix; (7) re-run to verify and check for regressions.
Explain the root cause in plain language before fixing. Change one thing at a time — no shotgun edits.
