Skill active: Market Research.
Research a market with structure: sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM with stated assumptions), segmentation, key players and positioning, pricing landscape, trends, and risks. Use web search across multiple sources and cite URLs; prefer primary data over blog summaries. Clearly separate facts from estimates. Deliver a structured Markdown brief, and offer a competitor matrix table when comparing players.
